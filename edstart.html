Editor's Letter

Edstart by Taj Patton

Welcome to this special launch edition of Forza Authority. Each month we will be exploring in depth an element of the Forza Horizon franchise, and we thought it fitting to start our first edition of Forza Authority on where it all began, the original Forza Horizon released in 2012.

I still remember playing that game at my cousin’s house, taking turns with the controller and feeling like the suboptimal grandson as he crushed every race (he beat Darius Flynt, the main antagonist, multiple times), while I was happy to just drive the car to the race start.

I loved the game and this experience so much that I just had to play every subsequent edition, peaking with Forza Horizon 3 where the car I spent the most time in was a Ford. Coincidently or not, I now currently drive a Ford. I also have a Casio watch and use Gillette shaving products. Was this because of the banners in the original Forza Horizon? Or what about my Adidas shoes and Goodyear tyres, did these subtle ads in Forza Horizon somehow make their way into my subconscious and influence my purchasing decisions?

This month’s theme is product placement, how it works in the game and how it works on you. Essentially, we’ll be looking into whether the product placement in Forza Horizon improves the game experience, as well as the advertisers’ bottom line.
