Feature: Business

Is Forza Horizon a Master of Business Acceleration?

We all know that Forza Horizon helps you to become a master of racing and acceleration, but is it also secretly a master of business acceleration through its sneaky product placements?

In short, yes. On top of its product placements increasing product awareness, it also improves brand awareness as well as purchase intention. I mean, I don’t think there is any Forza Horizon player that wouldn’t love to own Darius Flynt’s Ferrari 599XX in real life.

Forza Horizon is also a master of persuasion in the very way the game is designed. By being based in real life, it allows brands like Ferrari to not only advertise but be completely integrated and essential to the game’s immersion. Given how almost every racing game on the Xbox store incorporates brands proves that this is a winning formula. Even the very idea of a festival gives way to allowing in-game billboards, without taking away from the experience like those pop-up ads do in mobile games.

Importantly, this seamless integration of advertising in Forza Horizon is the key to advertising success. As mentioned before, those annoying pop-up ads feel out of place in a mobile game, and it’s these types of incongruent ads that actively fuel avoidance and reduce the effectiveness of a piece of communication.

So next time you’re racing in Forza Horizon and you see that Oakley billboard out of the interior of your Ferrari, just remember that whether you like it or not, you are being in fact being sold to.
